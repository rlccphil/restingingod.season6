Welcome to Season 6

Many people carry more than they were meant to carry — hurry, clutter, pressure, comparison, worry, and endless striving. Over time, the soul becomes heavy. This season is an invitation to live more lightly with God.

🌿 How to Use These Guidelines

  • Each tab above represents one Sabbath Day of the season. Navigate using the Day buttons.
  • Each day has three accordion sections: Before (prepare), During (practice), and After (reflect). Tap any section to open it.
  • Simplicity is not about having less for its own sake — it is about creating more space for peace, presence, and trust in God.
  • Use the Journal & Reflect area at the bottom of each day to write your personal reflections freely.
  • After journaling, you can email your reflection to yourself or a spiritual companion using the email feature.
  • You do not need to complete every practice perfectly. Receive each Sabbath as a gift, not a task.
  • Sabbath reminds us that life does not depend entirely on our effort, control, or accumulation — trust God.
🍃 Sabbath Day 26 · Season 6
"Therefore I tell you, do not be anxious about your life, what you will eat or what you will drink… Look at the birds of the air… your heavenly Father feeds them." Luke 12:22–31
  • Notice areas of physical, emotional, or mental clutter in your life.
  • Choose one small area you can simplify today.
✦ Prayer

"Lord, help me release what I do not truly need."

📖 Luke 12:22–31
  • Read Luke 12:22–31 slowly.
  • Spend time organizing or clearing a small space peacefully and unhurriedly.
  • Avoid buying or accumulating unnecessary things today.
  • Notice how simplicity affects your emotional state.
  • Spend time enjoying what you already have.
What kinds of excess weigh me down?
How did simplicity affect my peace today?
  • Thank God for His daily provision.
  • End your Sabbath with gratitude for enough.

☀️ Sabbath Day 27 · Season 6
"Therefore do not be anxious about tomorrow, for tomorrow will be anxious for itself. Sufficient for the day is its own trouble." Matthew 6:25–34
  • Acknowledge worries about the future honestly before God.
  • Release the need to solve everything immediately.
✦ Prayer

"Lord, teach me to trust You one day at a time."

📖 Matthew 6:25–34
  • Read Matthew 6:25–34 slowly.
  • Resist anxious planning throughout the day.
  • Whenever future worries arise, pause and breathe deeply.
  • Spend time doing something peaceful and grounding.
  • Practice staying present in today rather than living mentally in tomorrow.
What future concerns are hardest for me to release?
What helped me remain present today?
  • Entrust your future to God again in prayer.
  • Rest in the awareness that God cares for you.

🌸 Sabbath Day 28 · Season 6
"I have learned, in whatever situation I am, to be content. I can do all things through him who strengthens me." Philippians 4:11–13
  • Notice any feelings of dissatisfaction, comparison, or striving.
  • Avoid unnecessary scrolling or comparison before your Sabbath begins.
✦ Prayer

"Lord, help me receive today as enough."

📖 Philippians 4:11–13
  • Read Philippians 4:11–13 slowly.
  • Practice contentment throughout the day.
  • Enjoy ordinary things without needing more stimulation.
  • Resist comparing your life with others.
  • Spend time appreciating simple blessings already present in your life.
What makes it difficult for me to feel content?
What helped me experience enoughness today?
  • Thank God for sustaining you faithfully.
  • End your Sabbath peacefully.

📵 Sabbath Day 29 · Season 6
"Be still, and know that I am God. I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted in the earth!" Psalm 46
  • Decide ahead of time how you will reduce technology use today.
  • Inform others if necessary.
✦ Prayer

"Lord, help me become more attentive and less distracted."

📖 Psalm 46
  • Read Psalm 46 slowly.
  • Put away devices for extended periods if possible.
  • Notice how often you reach for stimulation automatically.
  • Spend time reading, walking, praying, resting, or talking with others.
  • Allow silence and slowness to return to your day.
What did I notice when technology became quieter?
How does constant stimulation affect my soul?
  • Ask God to help you live more attentively.
  • End the day with quiet stillness.

🌾 Sabbath Day 30 · Season 6
"He has told you, O man, what is good; and what does the Lord require of you but to do justice, and to love kindness, and to walk humbly with your God?" Micah 6:8
  • Begin your Sabbath slowly and intentionally.
  • Release urgency wherever possible.
✦ Prayer

"Lord, teach me to walk gently through this day."

📖 Micah 6:8
  • Read Micah 6:8 slowly.
  • Move through the day at a peaceful pace.
  • Speak gently to yourself and others.
  • Avoid forcing productivity or efficiency.
  • Take moments throughout the day to pause, breathe, and notice God's presence.
  • Let gentleness shape your words, thoughts, and actions.
What changes when I slow my pace?
Where do I need more gentleness in my life?
  • Thank God for moments of peace throughout the day.
  • Carry a slower spirit into the coming week.
