Season 6

Living Lightly

Learning Simplicity, Trust, and Freedom From Unnecessary Burdens

How to Use These Guidelines

Many people carry more than they were meant to carry. We fill our lives with hurry, clutter, pressure, comparison, worry, and endless striving. Over time, the soul becomes heavy.

This season is an invitation to live more lightly with God.

The Invitation

  • Slow your pace
  • Simplify your surroundings
  • Loosen your grip on control
  • Receive life as a gift

Simplicity

"Simplicity is not about having less for its own sake. It is about creating more space for peace, presence, and trust in God."

Your Daily Rhythm

B
BEFORE: Prepare your heart by identifying burdens, distractions, or pressures you may need to release.
D
DURING: Practice rhythms of simplicity, trust, contentment, and peaceful living.
A
AFTER: Reflect on what helped your soul feel lighter and freer.

"Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."

Matthew 11:28

Before

  • • Notice areas of physical, emotional, or mental clutter in your life.
  • "Lord, help me release what I do not truly need."
  • • Choose one small area you can simplify today.

During

  • • Read Luke 12:22-31 slowly.
  • • Spend time organizing or clearing a small space peacefully and unhurriedly.
  • • Avoid buying or accumulating unnecessary things today.
  • • Notice how simplicity affects your emotional state.
  • • Spend time enjoying what you already have.

After

  • Reflect:
  • What kinds of excess weigh me down?
  • How did simplicity affect my peace today?
  • • Thank God for His daily provision.
  • • End your Sabbath with gratitude for enough.

Before

  • • Acknowledge worries about the future honestly before God.
  • "Lord, teach me to trust You one day at a time."
  • • Release the need to solve everything immediately.

During

  • • Read Matthew 6:25-34 slowly.
  • • Resist anxious planning throughout the day.
  • • Whenever future worries arise, pause and breathe deeply.

After

  • What future concerns are hardest for me to release?
  • What helped me remain present today?
  • • Entrust your future to God again in prayer.
  • • Rest in the awareness that God cares for you.

Before

  • • Begin your Sabbath slowly and intentionally.
  • "Lord, teach me to walk gently through this day."

During

  • • Move through the day at a peaceful pace.
